How to Play

  1. 🎮 Press Free Play to be matched with a random player.
  2. 🤝 Or press Multiplayer and make a room for up to 8 friends.
  3. 🦠 Every time your opponent kills a germ, it sends a germ your way!
  4. 🏆 The person who gets rid of all their germs first wins!
  5. 📊 We count visits and games with a random ID on this device, to see how the game is doing. No ads, no tracking across sites.

HOW DO YOU WANT TO PLAY?

🎮 PRO CONTROLLER

  1. Open this computer's Bluetooth settings.
  2. Hold the small round sync button on top of the controller (next to the USB-C port) until the lights run back and forth.
  3. Choose Pro Controller in the Bluetooth list to connect it.
  4. Press any button on the controller (in Chrome, click CONNECT CONTROLLER and choose it).
